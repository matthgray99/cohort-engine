Live Exercise

The Margin Redesign

4 steps. 30 minutes. A pricing model built for profit.

You will need: Your compressed offer from Session 2, your P&L, and a calculator.

Step 01

Calculate Your TRUE Margin

Revenue minus ALL costs. Include your time at $500/hr.

Monthly Revenue $________
- COGS (direct costs) $________
- Fixed overhead (team, tools, rent) $________
- YOUR time (hrs x $500) $________
= TRUE Profit $________
= TRUE Margin % ______%

Most founders overestimate their margin by 15-25% because they do not cost their own time.

Step 02

Model Your 50%+ Price

What price hits 50% margin with your current delivery?

Total delivery cost per client: $_______

50% margin price = delivery cost x 2

60% margin price = delivery cost x 2.5

If the number feels uncomfortable, that is a sign you are underpricing now.

Step 03

Design Your Payment Terms

PIF Price

$_______ (10-15% discount from full price)

Payment Plan Price

$_______ total / $_______ per month x ___ months

PIF Incentive

Bonus, extra access, or discount?

Step 04

Identify 3 Costs to Cut

1

Tool or subscription to cancel

Which tool has no clear ROI? Kill it this week.

2

Custom process to systematize

What are you doing manually that should be templated?

3

Delivery step to eliminate

What do you deliver that clients do not actually value?

These three cuts go into effect this month. Not someday.

Case Study

Ruben Williams: $500 to $2K+

$500 in the bank. Selling a $20 digital product. Needed 5,000 sales per month just to survive. Same content. Same audience. Completely wrong economics.

Before $20 product price Need 5,000 sales/mo
After $2K+ product price Need 50 sales/mo

Lesson: Same content. Same audience. Restructured pricing changed the entire business model.
