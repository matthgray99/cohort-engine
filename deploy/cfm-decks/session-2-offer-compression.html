Live Exercise

The Offer Audit

4 steps. 30 minutes. One compressed offer.

You will need: Your current offer list, pricing, and the worksheet from Session 1.

Step 01

List All Current Offers

Every offer. Price. Margin. Write them all down.

Offer Price Margin 1:1 Coaching $________ ______% Group Program $________ ______% Digital Course $________ ______%

If you have more than 3 offers, the product tax is already hurting you.

Step 02

Score Against the Value Equation

Rate each offer 1-10 on each factor.

Problem Urgency: How painful is the problem it solves?

Solution Uniqueness: Can they get this anywhere else?

Proof: Do you have undeniable results to show?

The offer with the highest total score is your candidate for compression.

Step 03

Cut, Merge, or Raise

Cut

Low margin, low score. Kill it.

Merge

Overlap with another. Combine them.

Raise

High value, low price. Charge more.

Go through each offer right now. Decide its fate.

Case Study

Ali Abdaal: $5M on One Funnel

Had the audience. Millions of followers. But revenue was fragmented across courses, sponsorships, and low-ticket digital products.

1. Identified ONE core transformation his audience wanted

2. Built a single premium offer around that transformation

3. Created one funnel from content to offer

4. Systematized delivery so it scales without him

5. $5M funnel. One offer. Clear path.

Lesson: He had the audience all along. What he needed was the architecture.

Case Study

$100K/Month, 5 Offers to 1

Founder doing $100K/month spread across 5 different products. Each with different delivery, different support, different systems. Margin: 30%.

Before 30% margin 5 offers, 5 systems
After 55% margin 1 offer, 1 system

Same revenue. Almost double the profit. Half the complexity. That is compression.

One offer. One audience. One delivery system. Maximum margin.
