The Framework

The Hook Architecture

01

Pattern Interrupt

Stop the scroll. First 3 seconds or first line.

02

The Promise

What they get if they keep reading or watching.

03

The Proof

Why they should believe you.

04

The Payoff

Deliver on the promise. Every time.

01 / Hook Type

The Contrarian Hook

"Everything you know about [topic] is wrong."

Challenge a widely-held belief. Create cognitive tension. The reader HAS to find out why.

02 / Hook Type

The Story Hook

"3 years ago, I had $500 in my bank account..."

Open with a specific, vivid moment. Drop the reader into the middle of the action.

03 / Hook Type

The Data Hook

"92% of founders quit content in the first 90 days."

Lead with a surprising number. Specificity creates authority. Round numbers feel like guesses.

04 / Hook Type

The Question Hook

"What would change if your content generated 10 qualified leads per week?"

Ask a question they cannot answer without reading. Make it specific to their situation.

05 / Hook Type

The Confession Hook

"I spent $50K on a content team and fired them all."

Radical honesty creates instant trust. Vulnerability is the fastest path to authority.

The best content in the world is worthless if nobody stops to read it.
