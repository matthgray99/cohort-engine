The Framework

The Distribution Machine

Four layers. One system. Content goes out on schedule, engagement is captured, and everything gets measured.

01 Rhythm When to post, how often, per platform
02 Schedule Batch create, queue, auto-publish
03 Engage First 30 min, comments, DM triggers
04 Measure Impressions, engagement, DMs, clicks

Most founders skip layers 2 through 4. They create content but never build the machine that distributes, captures, and compounds it.

01 / Publishing Rhythm

When and Where You Show Up

Pick your primary platform (where your buyers already scroll)

Set frequency you can sustain for 90 days (not what sounds impressive)

Lock your posting times based on when your audience is active

The rhythm you can keep beats the schedule you cannot.

02 / Scheduling System

Batch. Queue. Forget.

Batch create all content in one sitting (1-2 hours)

Queue everything into your scheduling tool

Auto-publish at optimal times while you work on your business

The best content creators do not post in real time. They batch on Monday and the machine distributes all week.

03 / Engagement Protocol

The First 30 Minutes Matter Most

Be present for 30 minutes after every post goes live

Reply to every comment in the first hour

DM anyone who engages deeply (questions, shares, saves)

Engagement is not optional. It is the signal that tells the algorithm to push your content further.
