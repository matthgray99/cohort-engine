Key Concepts: Hook Anatomy (4 Layers)
Pattern Interrupt - Break the scroll. Say something unexpected, bold, or counterintuitive.
Relevance Signal - Immediately tell the reader "this is for you." Call out the audience or their problem.
Curiosity Gap - Open a loop. Create a question in their mind they need answered.
Value Promise - Tell them what they get if they keep reading, watching, or listening.
Section 1: The 5 Hook Types

Write 2 hooks for each type (10 total). Use your own voice, your own topics, your own audience.

Type 1: Contrarian (Challenge a common belief)

Example: "Posting every day is destroying your brand."
