Key Concepts: The 4 Brand Quadrants
Identity - Who you are, what you stand for, and why anyone should care.
Authority - Your credibility, proof, and positioning in the market.
Visibility - How consistently and clearly you show up across platforms.
Conversion - Whether your brand actually drives action, leads, and revenue.
