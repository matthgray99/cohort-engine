### Voice Calibration
- Builder language throughout: build, install, architect, engine, machine, system, waterfall
- Content-specific language: hooks, pillars, GPS, distribution, waterfall
- Direct and honest: no hype, no fake urgency
- Empathy without softness: "you know your content isn't where it should be"
- Specific over generic: every claim has a number attached

### Copywriting Standards Applied
- **Hopkins**: Specific claims (38 pieces from 1, 200+ hooks, 150,000 reach), reason-why copy
- **Ogilvy**: Every word earns its place, headline does 80% of the work

- **Hormozi**: Perceived value exceeds price by 10X ($800 vs. $3K-$10K/month agency), risk removal, time to value (14 days)
