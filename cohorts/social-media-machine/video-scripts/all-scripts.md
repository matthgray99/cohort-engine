## Production Notes

### Filming Guidelines
- **Hero Video**: Professional lighting, clean background (home office or studio). Energy is confident, not hype. Speak like you're talking to one founder who's frustrated with their content.
- **Course Intro**: Same setup as hero. Slightly warmer tone -- they already bought. Coach welcoming you to the team.
- **Case Study Throws**: Can be filmed in batch. Same energy as the YouTube content that precedes them. Seamless transition from the educational content to the CTA.
- **Session 6 Bonus**: Most important video. Film separately with intentional lighting and sound. Matt should be seated, not standing. Intimate, not performative. The early Founder OS content struggle story should land emotionally before the pivot to Velocity.

### Key Copy Rules Applied
- No em dashes (used -- throughout)
- Specific numbers: 100K followers, 38 pieces, 200+ hooks, $5,000, $800, 300 seats, 14 days, 6 sessions, 90 minutes, 1.5M followers
- Builder language: install, build, architect, engine, machine, system, waterfall
- Hopkins: specific claims, reason-why copy
- Ogilvy: every word earns its place
- Brunson: epiphany bridge (early Founder OS story), future pacing, value stacking
- Hormozi: perceived value exceeds price, risk removal ("don't decide now, just book a call")
