## Sequence Design Notes

### Voice & Tone Calibration
- **Email 1**: Warm, affirming, comprehensive. Validate the decision, eliminate confusion. Subconscious DFY seed via P.P.S. -- reads as wisdom, not pitch.
- **Email 2**: Direct, tactical, time-bound. One clear action with a simple framework. Implementation timeline contrast planted naturally within the pre-work context.
- **Email 3**: Coaching energy. Setting behavioral standards. "This is not a webinar." 6th insight introduces "installation by a team" concept without naming Velocity.
- **Email 4**: Community, belonging, social proof. DFY social proof woven into the cohort narrative -- past founders who had it built for them, others who built solo, both paths validated.
- **Email 5**: Focused energy. Anticipation. Direct but not pushy post-Session 6 hook creates open loop that resolves in the ascension sequence.

### DFY / Velocity Seeding Progression
1. **Email 1 (Subconscious)**: P.P.S. -- "The founders who get the most from this don't just learn the system -- they get it installed." No product name. No link. Pure identity framing.
2. **Email 2 (Subconscious)**: Implementation timeline contrast embedded in pre-work context. "6 months solo vs. one session" establishes the gap. "Imagine what's possible when you have a team doing the building for you" plants the seed without naming anything.
3. **Email 3 (Warm Awareness)**: 6th participation insight -- "they get it installed by a team that's done it thousands of times." Names the behavior, not the product. Forward reference to post-Session 6 reveal.
4. **Email 4 (Option Awareness + Social Proof)**: Past DFY clients referenced in cohort context. "Some founders will have their entire content machine, brand strategy, and distribution system built FOR them." Both paths validated. No pressure. Establishes that a path exists.
5. **Email 5 (Anticipation Hook)**: Direct but restrained. "One thing I'm going to share with you after Session 6..." Creates an open loop that keeps them attending all sessions and primes them for the post-cohort ascension sequence.

### Key Psychological Levers Used
1. **Identity installation** -- "You're a Social Media Machine founder now" (Email 1)
2. **Commitment escalation** -- Pre-work creates investment before Day 1 (Email 2)
3. **Behavioral framing** -- "This is not a webinar" sets the standard (Email 3)
4. **In-group belonging** -- Cohort stats and intros create the "room" (Email 4)
5. **Anticipatory emotion** -- "48 hours" creates excitement without fake urgency (Email 5)
6. **Loss framing** -- "The founders who show up cold spend the first session catching up" (Email 2)
7. **Endowed progress** -- Pre-work checklist makes them feel they've already started (Email 2)
8. **Social proof (aggregate)** -- Follower ranges, industry diversity, cohort size (Email 4)
9. **Curiosity gap** -- Post-Session 6 reveal teased but not explained (Emails 3, 5)
10. **Self-selection priming** -- "Both paths work" normalizes the DFY option without pushing (Email 4)

### Buyer's Remorse Mitigation
- Email 1 reframes the purchase: "installation, not a course"
- Email 2 activates investment: doing pre-work increases psychological commitment
- Email 3 raises the standard: participation framing makes them feel like they're part of something serious
- Email 4 shows the room: "these are real operators, not beginners" validates their decision
- Email 5 creates forward momentum: "48 hours" pushes attention toward the future, not the past purchase

### VIP Treatment Cadence
- Email 1: VIP benefits listed (WhatsApp, audit, group calls, priority hot seats)
- Email 3: VIP priority hot seat selection mentioned
- Email 4: WhatsApp activation reminder (June 27)
- Email 5: VIP priority in hot seat selection reiterated

### DFY Seeding Rules (Non-Negotiable)
- **Never name "Velocity" or "Founder OS" in pre-cohort emails.** The product reveal happens post-Session 6 in the ascension sequence.
- **Never include a link to an application or sales page.** The seeding is subconscious, then warm, then curious. No CTA for the upsell in this sequence.
- **Every seed must read as genuine insight, not a sales tease.** If it sounds like a marketer wrote it, cut it. If it sounds like Matt dropping real wisdom about implementation, keep it.
- **The progression must feel natural:** subconscious (1-2) -> warm awareness (3) -> option awareness + social proof (4) -> anticipation hook (5). Never jump ahead.
