## Executive Summary

The Social Media Machine is a 14-day live cohort (6 sessions, MWF, 90 min each) for founders who have real expertise and a business that works but whose content isn't converting, isn't consistent, or isn't systematized.

**Offer**: $800 standard / VIP tier (pricing TBD, likely $1,500-$2,000)
**Capacity**: 300 seats (100 VIP)
**Instructor**: Matt Gray (live, every session)
**Upsell target**: Founder OS Velocity ($29K PIF / $36K plan)
**Revenue target**: $240K gross (300 x $800) + VIP upside + Velocity conversions

This is Cohort #2 in the cohort engine (following Cash Flow Machine). Learnings from CFM Cohort #1 (May 29 - June 11) will be incorporated into SMM execution. The 19-day gap between CFM ending and SMM starting allows for iteration.

## Copy Requirements

### Landing Page
- Lead with the architecture framing: "Your content isn't converting because it lacks architecture, not creativity"
- VOC language: "I'm posting but nothing happens," "I don't know what to post," "content takes too long," "I have followers but no clients"
- Stack the value of each session with specific deliverables
- Hormozi risk reversal: $800 for a complete content machine vs. $5K+/month for a content agency
- No em dashes, WCAG 2.1 AA contrast

### Emails
- Subject lines: specific, curiosity-loaded, benefit-driven
- One primary CTA per email
- DFY/Velocity seeding follows the 5-stage progression
- CFM cross-sell email: bridge from cash flow architecture to content architecture naturally

### Video Scripts
- Hero video: name the content frustration, reframe as architecture problem, make the promise
- Course intro: set identity ("you're a Social Media Machine founder"), eliminate buyer's remorse
- Case study throws: specific transformations (2 posts/month to 38, 100K followers to $100K revenue)
- Session 6 bonus: Matt's own content journey as epiphany bridge, soft Velocity pitch
