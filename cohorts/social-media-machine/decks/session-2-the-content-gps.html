01 / Component

Platform Selection

The goal is not to be everywhere. The goal is to own 2-3 platforms so deeply that your audience cannot ignore you.

The Platform Selection Matrix

Audience
9/10
Engagement
7/10

4/10

Score each platform. High audience + high engagement + low effort = your platform.

One platform done well beats six platforms done poorly.

02 / Component

Content Pillars

Every piece of content you create should map to one of 3-5 pillars. If it does not fit a pillar, do not post it.

Every pillar leads back to your offer. Content without a commercial path is a hobby, not a strategy.

Pillar Examples

What Good Pillars Look Like

Frameworks: Teach a system. Builds authority.

Stories: Personal experience. Builds trust and connection.

Results: Client wins, data, proof. Builds credibility.

Behind the Scenes: Process, day-in-life. Builds relatability.

Contrarian Takes: Challenge the norm. Builds engagement.

Case Study

6 Platforms, Spread Thin, Zero Leads

Consultant posting randomly on LinkedIn, X, Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, and Facebook. 20 hours per week on content. 2 leads per month.

Before 2 leads / month 6 platforms, 20 hrs/week
After 8 leads / month 2 platforms, 8 hrs/week

Fix: Focused to LinkedIn + YouTube only. Output dropped 60%, leads went up 4X.
