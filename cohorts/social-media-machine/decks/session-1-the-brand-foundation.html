The Formula

Your Brand Positioning Statement

I help [WHO] achieve [WHAT] through [HOW]

WHO: Specific enough that they feel seen

WHAT: The outcome they want in their language

HOW: Your unique mechanism or system

Why It Matters

Every confused visitor is a lost client. Every clear positioning statement is a conversion machine.

What your bio needs to communicate about your value

Live Exercise

Open Your Worksheet

The Brand Foundation Diagnostic

30 minutes. Score your brand. Find the gaps. Write the statement that changes everything.

You will need: Your current bio, your offer description, and 30 minutes of radical honesty.

Step 01

Score Yourself 1-10 on All 16 Dimensions

Go through each dimension. Be brutally honest. Nobody sees this but you.

Dimension Score Mission__/10 Values__/10 Voice__/10 Story__/10

A 10 means world-class. A 1 means it does not exist yet.

Step 02

Identify Your 3 Weakest Dimensions

Look at your scores. Circle the 3 lowest numbers. These are your brand blind spots.

Your weakest dimension is the ceiling on your content performance. Fix it and everything above it improves.

Step 03

Write Your Brand Positioning Statement

I help [WHO] achieve [WHAT] through [HOW]

1

Bad: "I help entrepreneurs grow their business"

2

Better: "I help B2B founders hit $100K/mo"

3

Best: "I help B2B founders hit $100K/mo through organic content systems that run without them"

Step 04

Share Your Positioning Statement

Drop it in the chat. One sentence.

"I help [WHO] achieve [WHAT] through [HOW]."
