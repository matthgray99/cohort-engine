Live Exercise

Open Your Worksheet

The Sales Simplification Audit

20 minutes. Audit your process. Design your content. Simplify everything.

You will need: Your current sales process mapped out, your close rate, and your average sales cycle length.

Step 01

Map Your Current Sales Process

How many touches from lead to close? List every step.

Step Description Time 1 ___________________ ___ min 2 ___________________ ___ min 3 ___________________ ___ min 4 ___________________ ___ min

Total time from first touch to close: ___ hours.

Step 02

Identify What Content Could Replace

For each step, ask: could a piece of content do this instead?

Discovery call = deep-dive YouTube video

Follow-up = automated email sequence

Proposal review = case study landing page

Objection handling = FAQ content series

Circle every step that content could handle. Those are your cuts.

Step 03

Design 3 Pieces of Warm-Up Content

1

Objection Killer:

What is the #1 reason people do not buy? Create content that answers it.

2

Proof Piece:

A case study or transformation story that proves your system works.

3

Vision Piece:

Content that paints the picture of what life looks like after working with you.

Step 04

Simplify: Minimum Viable Sales Process

What is the fewest number of steps needed to close?

Write your simplified process. If it is more than 2 steps, ask why.
