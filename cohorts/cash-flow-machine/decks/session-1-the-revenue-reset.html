01 / Leak

Margin Compression

Raise price 20%, lose 10% of clients. You make more money with less work.

You feel guilty charging your current price

You over-deliver to justify what you charge

Revenue grows but profit stays flat

02 / Leak

Operational Inefficiency

Every dollar of overhead that does not directly produce revenue is a leak.

Monthly tool spend exceeds $2K with no clear ROI

Team members cannot explain what they shipped last week

You are the bottleneck in every decision

03 / Leak

Poor Offer Structure

ONE OFFER. MAX MARGIN.

Your team cannot explain your product lineup simply

04 / Leak

Cash Conversion Cycle

A $20K deal on a 12-month plan is $1,667/month of actual cash. That changes every decision.

You rely on new sales to fund last month's expenses

Payment plan defaults exceed 15%

Cash in bank does not reflect revenue on paper

Most models have holes.

Most founders stall because they use a Stage 2 model to run a Stage 4 business.

Stage 1
$0 - $10K/mo

Finding Product-Market Fit

Your job

Validate demand. Get 10 paying clients. Prove the offer works.

The trap

Building for 6 months before selling.

The unlock

Sell before you build. Let demand shape the product.

Stage 2
$10K - $50K/mo

First Systems, First Hires

Your job

Build repeatable delivery. Document the process.

The trap

Hiring before you have systems.

The unlock

Systemize yourself out of delivery before you hire anyone.

Stage 3
$50K - $100K/mo

Offer Optimization

Your job

Compress offers. Raise prices. Maximize margin per client.

The trap

Adding more offers instead of optimizing what works.

The unlock

One offer, one audience, one delivery system.

Stage 4
$100K - $500K/mo

Team and Operations

Your job

Build the team. Install the operating system. Remove yourself.

The trap

Scaling people without scaling systems.

The unlock

Every role has a scorecard. Every process has an owner.

Stage 5
$500K+/mo

Machine-Level Scale

Your job

Protect the machine. Optimize for cash flow, not revenue.

The trap

Chasing top-line growth instead of margin.

The unlock

Cash collected per employee is the number that matters.

Why Founders Stall

99% of founders stall at $100K/month.

Not because they lack hustle. Because they are running the wrong operating model for their revenue stage.

The Revenue Reset Diagnostic

30 minutes. ONE structural bottleneck. If fixed, it changes everything downstream.

You will need: Your P&L, your offer list, and 30 minutes of honest assessment.

Step 01

Audit Your Revenue Streams

List every offer. Price. Margin. % of revenue.

Offer Price Margin % Rev Coaching $15K 45% 60% Course $2K 80% 25% Done-for-you $5K 20% 15%

Which offer has the lowest margin? Circle it.

Step 02

Calculate Your Real Margin

Revenue minus ALL costs (COGS + fixed + your time at $500/hr) = Real Margin

Most founders overestimate margin by 15-25% because they do not cost their own time.

What is your REAL margin? Write the number.

Step 03

Name Your Cashflow Constraint

1

Margin Compression: I am selling too cheap

2

Operational Inefficiency: I am spending too much

3

Poor Offer Structure: I have too many offers

4

Cash Conversion: I am getting paid too slow

Circle ONE. This is the constraint we fix over 13 days.

Step 04

Share Your Constraint

Drop it in the chat. One sentence.

"My constraint is [X] because [Y]."

Case Study

The $500K Business Keeping 10%

Doing $500K/year. After team, tools, and over-serving clients who paid $3K for $15K of value, he was keeping $50K.

Before $50K kept / year 4 offers, 10% margin
After $240K kept / year 1 offer, 48% margin

Diagnosis: Margin Compression + Poor Offer Structure

Fix: Compressed 4 offers to 1. Raised the price.
