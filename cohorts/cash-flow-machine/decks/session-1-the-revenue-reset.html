Leak 01

Margin Compression

You are selling too cheap for what you deliver.

If you raised your price 20% and lost 10% of clients, you would make more money with less work.

Warning signs:

You feel guilty charging your current price

You over-deliver to justify what you charge

Revenue grows but profit stays flat

Leak 02

Operational Inefficiency

Too much overhead. Too many moving parts.

Every dollar of overhead that does not directly produce revenue is a leak. Most founders have 3-5 tools and 2-3 team members they do not actually need.

Warning signs:

Monthly tool spend exceeds $2K with no clear ROI

Team members cannot explain what they shipped last week

You are the bottleneck in every decision

Leak 03

Poor Offer Structure

Wrong product mix creating complexity without margin.

Every offer you add is a tax on your time, team, and margin. The question is not "what else can I sell?" It is "what should I stop selling?"

Warning signs:

You have 3+ offers at different price points

Each offer requires different delivery systems

Your team cannot explain your product lineup simply

Leak 04

Cash Conversion Cycle

Getting paid too slow, spending too fast.

Revenue recognized is not cash collected. A $20K deal on a 12-month payment plan is $1,667/month of actual cash. That changes every decision.

Warning signs:

You rely on new sales to fund last month's expenses

Payment plan defaults exceed 15%

Cash in the bank does not reflect revenue on paper

Every dollar you earn passes through your business model before it reaches your pocket. Most models have holes.

The Map

The 5 Revenue Stages

Stage 1 $0-$10K/mo Finding product-market fit
Stage 2 $10K-$50K/mo First systems, first hires
Stage 3 $50K-$100K/mo Offer optimization
Stage 4 $100K-$500K/mo Team and operations
Stage 5 $500K+/mo Machine-level scale

Most founders stall because they use a Stage 2 model to run a Stage 4 business.

Stage 1
$0 - $10K/mo

Finding Product-Market Fit

Your job

Validate demand. Get 10 paying clients. Prove the offer works.

The trap

Spending 6 months building before selling. Perfecting instead of shipping.

The unlock

Sell before you build. Let demand shape the product.

Stage 2
$10K - $50K/mo

First Systems, First Hires

Your job

Build repeatable delivery. Make your first hire. Document the process.

The trap

Hiring before you have systems. Paying someone to figure out what you have not defined.

The unlock

Systemize yourself out of delivery before you hire anyone.

Stage 3
$50K - $100K/mo

Offer Optimization

Your job

Compress your offers. Raise prices. Maximize margin per client.

The trap

Adding more offers to grow instead of optimizing the ones that work.

The unlock

One offer, one audience, one delivery system. Ruthless simplicity.

Stage 4
$100K - $500K/mo

Team and Operations

Your job

Build the team. Install the operating system. Remove yourself from day-to-day.

The trap

Scaling people without scaling systems. Growing headcount instead of output per person.

The unlock

Every role has a scorecard. Every process has an owner. You lead, not manage.

Stage 5
$500K+/mo

Machine-Level Scale

Your job

Protect the machine. Optimize for cash flow, not revenue. Build leverage.

The trap

Chasing top-line growth instead of margin. Acquiring customers that cost more than they return.

The unlock

Revenue is a vanity metric. Cash collected per employee is the number that matters.

Why Founders Stall

99% of founders stall at $100K/month.

Not because they lack hustle. Because they are running the wrong operating model for their revenue stage. Doing more of the same does not scale. The model has to change.

Work Harder Change the Architecture

You don't have a revenue problem. You have an architecture problem.

Live Exercise

Open Your Worksheet

The Revenue Reset Diagnostic

In the next 30 minutes, you are going to identify your ONE Cashflow Constraint. Not a list of problems. ONE structural bottleneck that, if fixed, changes everything downstream.

You will need: Your P&L, your offer list, and 30 minutes of honest assessment.

Step 01

Audit Your Revenue Streams

List every offer. Price. Estimated margin. Percentage of total revenue.

Offer Price Margin % Rev Coaching $15K 45% 60% Course $2K 80% 25% Done-for-you $5K 20% 15%

Which offer has the lowest margin? Circle it.

Step 02

Calculate Your Real Margin

Revenue minus ALL costs (COGS + fixed costs + your time valued at $500/hr) = Real Margin

Most founders overestimate their margin by 15-25% because they do not cost their own time.

What is your REAL margin? Write the number.

Step 03

Name Your Cashflow Constraint

1

Margin Compression: I am selling too cheap

2

Operational Inefficiency: I am spending too much

3

Poor Offer Structure: I have too many offers

4

Cash Conversion: I am getting paid too slow

Circle ONE. This is the constraint we fix over the next 13 days.

Step 04

Share Your Constraint

Drop your constraint in the chat. One sentence.

"My constraint is [X] because [Y]."

Hearing 50 founders name their constraint in real time is one of the most powerful moments in this cohort. You will realize you are not alone, and you will see patterns you did not expect.

Case Study

The $500K Business Keeping 10%

A founder came to me doing $500K/year. Impressive top line. But after team, tools, delivery costs, and over-serving clients who paid $3K for $15K of value, he was keeping $50,000.

Diagnosis

Margin Compression + Poor Offer Structure

The Fix

Compressed from 4 offers to 1. Raised the price. Reduced delivery complexity.

Within 6 months: same revenue, 48% margin.

Case Study

From $4M/Year and Broken
