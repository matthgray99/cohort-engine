01 / Core Move

The Revenue Move

What is the single action that would have the biggest impact on your revenue in the next 90 days?

Not 10 things. ONE. The one that, if you executed it well, would make the other 9 unnecessary.

02 / Core Move

The Systems Move

What is the one system that, once installed, removes you as the bottleneck?

Content system. Sales process. Delivery workflow. Pick the one where your absence would break things.

03 / Core Move

The Team Move

Every 90-day plan eventually breaks at team execution. Who do you need?

One hire. One role change. One conversation. The team move is where the plan meets reality.

Build Your 90-Day Plan

01 / Step

Name Your 3 Core Moves

Revenue Move: _______________

Systems Move: _______________

Team Move: _______________

Be specific. "Grow revenue" is not a move. "Launch compressed $15K offer by Week 4" is.

02 / Step

Set Weekly Milestones

For each move, define what "done" looks like at Week 4, Week 8, and Week 12.

If you cannot describe the milestone, the move is too vague. Sharpen it.

03 / Step

Identify the ONE Hire

Who is the one person, role change, or conversation that unlocks your 90-day plan?

Most plans break at team. Name the person now.

04 / Step

Share Your #1 Move
