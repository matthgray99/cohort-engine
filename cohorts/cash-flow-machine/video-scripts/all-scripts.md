## Production Notes

### Filming Guidelines
- **Hero Video**: Professional lighting, clean background (home office or studio). Energy is confident, not hype. Speak like you're talking to one person who's on the fence.
- **Course Intro**: Same setup as hero. Slightly warmer tone -- they already bought. This is a coach welcoming you to the team.
- **Case Study Throws**: Can be filmed in batch. Same energy as the YouTube content that precedes them. Seamless transition.
- **Session 6 Bonus**: This is the most important video. Film it separately with intentional lighting and sound. Matt should be seated, not standing. Intimate, not performative. The Bitmaker story should land emotionally before the pivot to Velocity.

### Key Copy Rules Applied
- No em dashes (used -- throughout)
- Specific numbers: $20K-$500K, $800, 300 seats, 14 days, 6 sessions, 90 minutes, $13.8M, 18,000 founders
- Builder language: install, build, architect, engine, machine, system
- Hopkins: reason-why copy, specific claims
- Ogilvy: every word earns its place
- Brunson: epiphany bridge (Bitmaker story), future pacing, value stacking
- Hormozi: perceived value exceeds price, risk removal ("don't decide now, just book a call")
