## Copy Notes

### Voice Calibration
- Builder language throughout: build, install, architect, engine, machine, system
- Direct and honest: no hype, no fake urgency, no motivation-speak
- Empathy without softness: "you know the numbers aren't where they should be"
- Specific over generic: every claim has a number attached

### Copywriting Standards Applied
- **Hopkins**: Specific claims, reason-why copy, one clear proposition
- **Ogilvy**: Every word earns its place, headline does 80% of the work
- **Brunson**: Story-driven (case studies), value stacking (6 sessions), future pacing (life after 14 days)
- **Hormozi**: Perceived value exceeds price by 10X, risk removal (conditional guarantee), time to value (14 days)
