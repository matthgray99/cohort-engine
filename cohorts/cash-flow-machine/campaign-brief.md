**Offer**: $800 standard / VIP tier (pricing TBD, likely $1,500-$2,000)
**Capacity**: 300 seats (100 VIP)
**Instructor**: Matt Gray (live, every session)
**Upsell target**: Founder OS Velocity ($29K PIF / $36K plan)
**Revenue target**: $240K gross (300 x $800) + VIP upside + Velocity conversions

This is Cohort #1. The primary goals are: fill the room, deliver a transformational experience, and convert 15-25% of attendees into Velocity within 30 days post-cohort.

## Copy Requirements

### Landing Page
- Hopkins-level specificity: name the numbers, the timeline, the exact transformation
- Ogilvy brevity: every word earns its place
- Brunson stacking: show what each session delivers, stack the total value
- Hormozi risk reversal: make the $800 feel like a rounding error
- VOC language in pain blocks: pull from actual founder language (DM surveys, call transcripts, intake forms)
- No em dashes
- WCAG 2.1 AA contrast on all text

### Emails
- Subject lines: specific, curiosity-loaded, benefit-driven
- One primary CTA per email
- Emotional specificity: name the feeling, not just the problem
- P.S. lines are the second-most-read part after subject lines
- DFY/Velocity seeding follows the 5-stage progression (subconscious -> warm awareness -> option awareness -> anticipation -> full pitch)

### Video Scripts
- Hero video: stop the scroll, name the pain, make the promise, drive enrollment
- Course intro: eliminate buyer's remorse, set behavioral expectations
- Case study throws: bridge from content to enrollment via specific founder transformations
- Session 6 bonus: Bitmaker story as epiphany bridge, soft Velocity pitch
