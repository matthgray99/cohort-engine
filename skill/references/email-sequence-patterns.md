# Email Sequence Patterns

## 10-File Structure (every cohort generates all 10)

### 00 - Behavioral Science Layer
Meta-document defining the psychological principles applied across all sequences:
- Identity installation ("You are a [Cohort Name] founder now")
- Commitment escalation (pre-work creates investment before Day 1)
- Loss framing ("The founders who show up cold spend the first session catching up")
- Social proof (cohort stats, revenue ranges, industry diversity)
- Endowed progress (pre-work checklist makes them feel they've started)
- Anticipatory emotion ("48 hours" creates excitement without fake urgency)

### 01 - Waitlist Warmup (5 emails)
Pre-launch sequence building anticipation:
1. Announcement (problem awareness)
2. The vision (what becomes possible)
3. Social proof (who's already interested)
4. Early bird / founding member pricing
5. Last chance to join waitlist

### 02 - Launch Enrollment (7 emails)
Cart-open sequence:
1. The Problem (entry-level pain, not deep diagnosis)
2. The Margin Angle (structural problem framing)
3. The Simplicity Angle (offer tax concept)
4. Case Study (real founder transformation with numbers)
5. The System (what they get, session by session)
6. Social Proof + Urgency (cohort filling up)
7. Last Call (deadline + VIP mention)

### 03 - Cart Close Urgency (5 emails)
Final 48-72 hours:
1. 48 hours left
2. 24 hours left (most effective email in the sequence)
3. Tonight at midnight
4. Final hours (morning of close)
5. Closed (waitlist for next cohort)

### 04 - Pre-Cohort Onboarding (5 emails)
Post-purchase, pre-Session 1:
1. Welcome (identity confirmation, timeline, VIP details)
2. Pre-work (specific preparation checklist)
3. 10X value (behavioral expectations, participation rules)
4. Meet your cohort (social proof, community intro)
5. Final prep (48 hours out, logistics, anticipation)

DFY seeding progression (subconscious, never name the product):
- E1: "installed" language
- E2: DIY time contrast
- E3: "installation by a team" insight
- E4: Social proof of DFY clients
- E5: Open loop for post-Session 6 reveal

### 05 - During Cohort Sessions (12 emails)
Pre + post each session:
- Pre-session: reminder, what to bring, hot seat submission
- Post-session: key takeaway, homework reminder, community prompt

### 06 - Post-Cohort Ascension (7 emails)
Upsell to flagship/Velocity:
1. Celebration + seed (what you built in 14 days)
2. The gap (blueprint vs installation, 92% of plans die)
3. The bridge (what Velocity actually is)
4. Objection killer ("I can't add another thing" -- we remove, not add)
5. Social proof (Velocity client transformations)
6. The math ($107K+ individually vs Velocity)
7. Last call (deadline, urgency, final CTA)

### 07 - No-Show Re-engagement (5 emails)
For enrolled members who stop attending:
1. Missed you (personal, not guilt-tripping)
2. Replay available (low-friction re-entry)
3. What you missed (FOMO via specific takeaways)
4. Still time (sessions remaining, catch-up path)
5. Final check-in (offer support, ask what's blocking them)

### 08 - Abandoned Cart (4 emails)
For people who started checkout but didn't complete:
1. Still thinking? (address top objection)
2. Here's what you'd miss (session-by-session preview)
3. Social proof (who already enrolled)
4. Final chance (deadline + scarcity)

### 09 - VIP Upgrade (3 emails)
For standard enrollees to upgrade to VIP:
1. What VIP includes (specific benefits)
2. The math (ROI of personal access vs standard)
3. Last chance to upgrade (deadline before cohort starts)

## Format Per Email
```markdown
**Send**: [Date/trigger]
**Purpose**: [One sentence]
**Subject line**: [Primary]
**Preview text**: [Companion to subject]
---
[Body copy]
---
```

## Copywriting Rules
- Matt Gray's voice: builder language, direct, no fluff
- Every sentence earns the next
- Hopkins: specific claims, tested headlines
- Ogilvy: every word justifies its place
- Brunson: story-driven, value stacking
- Hormozi: perceived value > price
- No em dashes (use -- or reword)
- ONE primary CTA per email
- P.S. lines are second-most-read after subject
