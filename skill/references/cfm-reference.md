# Cash Flow Machine - Reference Implementation

## Overview
- **Name**: Cash Flow Machine
- **Sessions**: 6, MWF over 14 days, 90 minutes each
- **Audience**: Founders doing $20K-$500K+/month with revenue volatility
- **Price**: $800 standard, VIP available ($50K+/month founders)
- **Capacity**: 300 seats (100 VIP)
- **Cohort #1**: May 29 - June 11, 2026
- **Upsell target**: Founder OS Velocity ($29K PIF / $36K plan)

## Session Architecture
| # | Title | Framework | Case Studies |
|---|-------|-----------|-------------|
| 1 | The Revenue Reset | 4 Revenue Leaks, 5 Revenue Stages | $500K/10% founder, Matt Bitmaker |
| 2 | Offer Compression | Magnetic Offer (4 Principles), Value Equation | Ali Abdaal $5M, 20@$5K math |
| 3 | Pricing & Margin Expansion | 50%+ Margin Thinking, Pricing Ladder | Ruben Williams $20->$2K, Matt $4M->$13.8M |
| 4 | The Lead Engine | Personal Media Company, 4-Layer Stack | Alex Ikonn $15M, Brand OS client |
| 5 | Sales Simplification | Content CEO Playbook, Trust Ladder | 4-call to 1-call founder, Matt's system |
| 6 | 90-Day Execution Plan | 3 Core Moves, Weekly Cadence | Velocity client $40K->$120K, Matt's story |

## Homework (1 item per session)
1. Complete the Revenue Reset Worksheet
2. Complete the Offer Compression Worksheet
3. Complete the Pricing & Margin Expansion Worksheet
