# Velocity Pitch Progression

## The Arc Across 6 Sessions

The Velocity/DFY pitch builds gradually across the cohort. The goal: by Session 6, every founder in the room understands that having their systems BUILT by a team is the logical next step. 100% conversion target.

### Sessions 1-3: Subconscious Seeding
**Rule**: Never name the product. Never include a CTA or link. Every seed must read as genuine founder insight, not a pitch.

- Session 1: "The best founders don't just learn the system. They get it <squiggly>installed</squiggly>."
- Session 2: "What we just did in 90 minutes took most founders 6 months to figure out alone. Imagine if you didn't have to do it <squiggly>alone</squiggly>."
- Session 3: "Your pricing model is now 10X better. Imagine if your content engine, brand strategy, and sales system got the same <squiggly>treatment</squiggly>."

**Psychology**: Plant the idea of "having it done for you" as an identity concept, not a product. Founders start self-selecting: "Am I someone who builds alone, or someone who gets it installed?"

### Sessions 4-5: Warm Pitch
**Rule**: Name the concept (not necessarily the product). Include a booking CTA. Frame it as an option, not a push.

- Session 4 (Lead Engine): Full slide after case studies.
  - "What if we built your entire lead engine FOR you?"
  - Brief overview: brand strategy, content GPS, 200+ hooks, all built in 30 days
  - "Book a call with my team to get your content strategy mapped."
  - [BOOKING LINK PLACEHOLDER]

- Session 5 (Sales Simplification): Full slide + CTA.
  - "You now have the sales framework. The question isn't whether it works. It is who is going to <squiggly>implement</squiggly> it."
  - Velocity overview: 6 months, C-suite team, we build 80%/you add 20%
  - [BOOKING LINK PLACEHOLDER]

**Psychology**: The teaching content naturally creates the awareness gap. "I now know what to do. I don't have time to do it all." The pitch fills that gap.

### Session 6: Full Conversion Sequence (3-4 slides)
**Rule**: Full Jason Fladlien-level conversion. Confident, not desperate. "This is the logical next step" energy.

Slide sequence:
1. **Pattern Interrupt**: "You have the plan. But here is what I know about plans. 92% die within 30 days."
2. **The Solution**: "What if you didn't have to build it alone?" -- 4 description cards showing what Velocity does
3. **The Math**: Animated counter $107K+ if hired individually. "We build it in 30 days." Graduate pricing + PIF bonus.
4. **Social Proof**: 3 client transformation cards with before/after numbers
5. **CTA**: "Book your strategy call before you leave this <squiggly>Zoom</squiggly>." + urgency deadline

**Psychology**: The 14-day cohort proved the system works. The only question left is implementation speed. Velocity answers that question.

## Worksheet DFY Pitches (session-specific)
Each worksheet ends with a half-page pitch that relates to that session's content:
- WS01: "Identified your revenue leak? Imagine if we fixed it for you in 30 days."
- WS02: "Compressed your offer? What if we built the content system to sell it?"
- WS03: "Pricing dialed? Imagine if your content, brand, and sales got the same treatment."
- WS04: "Mapped your lead engine? We build it. In 30 days. Trained on your voice."
- WS05: "Sales process simpler? What if your content did the selling 24/7?"
- WS06: "You have the plan. We have the team. Book a call."

## Email Seeding (pre-cohort)
The 04-pre-cohort-onboarding emails seed the concept subconsciously:
- Email 1: "installed" language
- Email 2: DIY time contrast
- Email 3: "installation by a team" as 6th insight
- Email 4: "Some founders will have everything built FOR them"
- Email 5: "Something after Session 6 that changes everything"
Never name Velocity in pre-cohort emails.
